The approved technical design is at `docs/specs/2026-08-04-auth-design.md` and covers:

Requirements:
- Users can register with email/password
- Users can log in and receive a JWT token
- Protected routes require valid JWT
- Tokens expire after 24 hours
- Support password reset via email

Turn this approved design into a multi-step implementation plan. It involves the user model, auth routes, middleware, and email integration.
